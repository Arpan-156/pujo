# Burdwan Pujo - Chatbot Knowledge Base

## 1. General Festival Information
- **Festival Name**: Durga Puja 2026
- **Start Date**: Sat Oct 17 2026
- **Stages / Important Dates**:
  - **Mahalaya** (মহালয়া): Sat 10 Oct. Ritual: Tarpan, and the voice on the radio. Before dawn, families offer tarpan to their ancestors and the old Mahishasura Mardini recital fills the house. The Goddess has been invited.
  - **Panchami** (পঞ্চমী): Fri 16 Oct. Ritual: Final touches and early hopping. The calm before the festival. Artisans add final brushstrokes, lighting trials illuminate the streets, and crowds begin hopping to beat the rush.
  - **Shashthi** (ষষ্ঠী): Sat 17 Oct. Ritual: Bodhon: the face is unveiled. At dusk the idol's face is revealed and the Goddess is welcomed with conch shells and dhak. Pandals open their gates for the first night.
  - **Saptami** (সপ্তমী): Sun 18 Oct. Ritual: Nabapatrika snan. The banana plant wrapped in a red-bordered sari, Kola Bou, is bathed in the river at first light and carried to the pandal.
  - **Ashtami** (অষ্টমী): Mon 19 Oct. Ritual: Anjali and Sandhi Puja. The busiest day. Morning anjali with the whole neighbourhood, then Sandhi Puja at the hinge between Ashtami and Navami, lit by 108 lamps.
  - **Navami** (নবমী): Tue 20 Oct. Ritual: Maha aarti and dhunuchi naach. Smoke, coconut husk and dhak. Dancers balance dhunuchis on their hands, on their teeth, and performs the famous dhunuchi naach.
  - **Dashami** (দশমী): Wed 21 Oct. Ritual: Sindoor khela and Bishorjon. Married women smear sindoor on the Goddess and on each other. By evening the idols move toward the water, and the dhak slows.

## 3. Themes
- **Heritage Bengal** (ঐতিহ্যের বাংলা): Terracotta panels, atchala roofs, zamindari courtyards. Pandals that rebuild the Bengal our grandparents remember.
- **Mythology** (পুরাণকথা): The Devi Mahatmya told in light, paper and bamboo. Battle scenes, cosmic oceans, the ten mahavidyas.
- **Contemporary Art** (সমকালীন শিল্প): Installations that borrow from galleries: suspended forms, mirrored surfaces, sculpture at street scale.
- **Social Awareness** (সমাজভাবনা): Pandals that carry a message: water, women, waste, health, the quiet workers of the city.
- **Architecture** (স্থাপত্য): Replicas of gates, stations and palaces, built at full size from bamboo, cloth and plywood in three weeks.
- **Rural Bengal** (গ্রামবাংলা): Mud walls, thatch, palm and paddy. The village as memory, rebuilt inside the town.
- **International Concepts** (বিশ্বভাবনা): Cathedrals, canals and far-off temples reimagined around a Bengali goddess.
- **Eco-friendly** (পরিবেশবান্ধব): Clay idols, jute, bamboo, dried leaves. Pandals designed to go back to the earth after Dashami.
- **Abstract Art** (বিমূর্ত): Colour and form with no literal story. Stand inside it and decide what you see.
